Timers & automation

Countdown and sleep timers run on the ESP32. Scheduled ON/OFF uses India Standard Time (IST) from NTP while Wi-Fi is connected.

Daily schedule · IST

Timers ready when MQTT is connected.

Scheduled ON turns on channels that are currently off at full brightness; channels already on retain their current brightness. Scheduled OFF turns both channels off. The schedule repeats daily. Countdown and sleep timers are not retained across ESP32 power loss.
